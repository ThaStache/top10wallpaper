Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about getting your data and making your wallpaper.

Is Top Ten Wall free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy and no account to create.

Is my listening history uploaded anywhere?

No. Your file is read by your own browser and never leaves your device. The only things sent over the internet are album and artist names, so cover art can be found. Nothing is saved after you close the page.

How do I get my Apple Music data?
  1. Go to privacy.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
  2. Choose Request a copy of your data.
  3. Select Apple Media Services information and submit the request.
  4. When Apple emails you that it's ready, download the zip.
How long does Apple take to send my data?

Usually a few days. Apple says it can take up to seven days to verify the request and prepare your files.

Which file do I upload?

Drop the zip Apple sends you exactly as it is. Top Ten Wall finds the right file inside it automatically. If you'd rather unzip it yourself, the file you need is Apple Music Play Activity.csv.

It says my file doesn't include album names. What happened?

You probably uploaded Apple Music Play History Daily Tracks.csv. That file lists songs but not albums. Use Apple Music Play Activity.csv from the same export, or just drop the whole zip.

How are albums ranked?

You can choose. Plays counts a song as played when you listened for at least 30 seconds or half its length. Time listened adds up every minute you spent on an album, including partial listens.

Why is a cover missing or wrong?

Covers are matched by album and artist name from public catalogs, and some titles are hard to match, such as live albums, deluxe editions, or albums with common names. Use Add cover next to any album to pick the right image from your device.

Why is an audiobook or soundtrack in my top ten?

Your history includes everything you played in the Music app. Use Hide next to any album to remove it, and the next album moves up.

Why are some years missing?

Years appear only when your export has plays from them. Your history starts when you began using Apple Music, so earlier years won't show up.

What screen sizes are available?

HD (1920 × 1080), QHD (2560 × 1440), 4K (3840 × 2160), MacBook (2880 × 1800), ultrawide (3440 × 1440), and super ultrawide (5120 × 2160). If you're not sure, choose the largest size. It will scale down cleanly.

How do I set it as my wallpaper?
  • Mac: open System Settings, go to Wallpaper, click Add Photo, and choose the PNG you saved.
  • Windows: right-click the saved PNG and choose Set as desktop background.
Does it work with Spotify?

Not yet. Spotify support is coming soon, using the listening history you can download from your Spotify account.

Can I share or sell my wallpaper?

Share it all you like for personal use. Please don't sell it or print it for sale, because album artwork belongs to the artists and labels who made it.

Is this made by Apple?

No. Top Ten Wall is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
