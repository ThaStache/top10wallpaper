About Top Ten Wall

A free tool that turns your Apple Music listening history into a desktop wallpaper of the ten albums you played most.

Why it exists

Year-end recaps are fun for a week, then they disappear into a story or a screenshot. Your most played albums say a lot about a year, so this puts them somewhere you'll actually see them every day: your desktop.

Pick a single year to remember a specific stretch of your life, or choose all time to see the albums that have stuck with you the longest.

How it works

  1. You request a copy of your data from Apple at privacy.apple.com. It includes a full log of everything you've played.
  2. You drop that file onto Top Ten Wall. Your browser reads it and counts plays and listening time for every album, by year and all time.
  3. Album covers are looked up from public music catalogs (the iTunes Search API, MusicBrainz, and the Cover Art Archive).
  4. You choose a layout, background, and screen size, then save the image as a PNG.

Your data stays on your device

Your listening history is processed entirely in your browser. It isn't uploaded, stored, or shared, and there are no accounts. When you close the tab, it's gone. The only things sent anywhere are album and artist names, so the site can look up their cover art.

What's next

Spotify support is on the way, using Spotify's own data export. Have an idea or found a bug? Get in touch below.
